# Super Admin Feature

## Administrator owns final technician decisions
Staff may mark technician applications under review and request information resubmission. Only superadmin Administrator accounts may approve or reject technician applications or suspend, ban, and restore technician accounts. Enforce these limits inside Livewire actions, not only by hiding controls.
